### Added
- Initial release. Versioning and release-documentation discipline:
  SemVer decision tree (breaking then feature then fix), pre-1.0 rules,

  Keep a Changelog workflow built on a permanent Unreleased section,
  atomic release steps (bump, changelog, README, tag), MAJOR confirmation
